# UC-05 — Policy lifecycle and acknowledgement

## Situation

Security policies and procedures must exist, be approved, be mapped to controls, be acknowledged by staff, and be periodically reviewed.

## Actors

- Policy owners (security, HR, legal)
- Approvers (multi-step paths)
- All in-scope staff
- Auditors verifying acknowledgement evidence

## Trigger

Program setup, annual review cycle, new hires, framework additions, or policy change.

## Flow

1. **Create** — Use Sprinto templates (acceptable use, access control, BCP/DR, incident management, vendor management, SDLC, …), AI-assisted drafting, or upload existing documents; sync from Confluence / Google Drive / Docs.
2. **Review & approve** — In-line comments; single or multi-approval paths; version history and custom versioning.
3. **Map** — Link policies to controls (AI suggestions) for evidence and reporting.
4. **Distribute** — Policy registers by zone; acknowledgement campaigns per employee group; acknowledgement in Slack or Employee Portal.
5. **Monitor** — Policy monitoring checks for review cadence and overdue acknowledgements; track outcomes and per-employee history.
6. **Brand & export** — Branded PDFs for auditors or customers.

## Sprinto capabilities used

Policies module · approval paths · policy registers · acknowledgement campaigns · Slack/Teams integration · Confluence/Drive sync · policy monitoring checks.

## Outcome

Audit-ready policy set with traceable approvals and acknowledgement evidence; reduced chasing.
