# UC-01 — First certification (SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / HIPAA)

## Situation

A startup or small company has no dedicated compliance function. An enterprise prospect, partner or regulator requires a SOC 2 report or ISO 27001 certificate. Sprinto positions itself as "your first compliance operator".

## Actors

- Founder / CTO / Head of Engineering (program owner)
- Engineers and IT (remediation)
- All staff (policies, training, devices)
- External auditor from Sprinto network or BYOA (Growth tier)

## Trigger

Customer security requirement, procurement blocker, or go-to-market into a regulated segment.

## Flow

1. **Scope** — Select framework(s); define products/URLs, in-scope staff, infra and devices. SOC 2 Launchpad / self-serve dashboard generates a blueprint.
2. **Connect** — Integrate cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure), IdP (Google Workspace/Entra/Okta), HRIS, VCS, MDM or deploy Dr. Sprinto.
3. **Gap analysis** — Automated checks surface failing controls; AI maps template policies and controls.
4. **Remediate** — Tasks assigned with SLAs; policies approved and acknowledged; training launched; devices hardened.
5. **Collect evidence** — Automated evidence plus workflow checks for manual items (pentest, reviews, BCP tests).
6. **Audit** — Create audit, select partner, share evidence; auditor reviews in-app; findings tracked.

## Sprinto capabilities used

Pre-mapped frameworks and baselines · policy templates · 300+ integrations · automated and workflow checks · People onboarding · Trainings · Staff Devices · Evidence library · Audit management · Auditor network.

## Outcome

Audit readiness "in weeks"; first SOC 2 Type I or ISO 27001 Stage 1/2 with minimal spreadsheet effort; non-technical staff can complete tasks via Employee Portal.
