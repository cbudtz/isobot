# UC-03 — Multi-entity and regional programs (Zones)

## Situation

The company operates multiple business units, products, subsidiaries or regions with partially different obligations, owners and risk profiles.

## Actors

- Group GRC / CISO office
- Entity or regional compliance owners
- Auditors with entity-specific scope

## Trigger

M&A, new region (e.g. EU + US), product lines with different frameworks, or auditors requiring segregated evidence.

## Flow

1. **Define zones** — Create zones per business unit/region in Settings; map workflow checks, policies, risks, vendors to zones.
2. **Scope entities** — Staff, infra, repos and devices assigned per zone; registers (policy, vendor, risk) partitioned by zone.
3. **Operate** — Dashboards and task views filtered by zone; zone-specific risk assessments and acknowledgement campaigns.
4. **Aggregate** — Group-level control health and risk heatmaps; risk inheritance and propagation across units (ERM add-on).
5. **Audit** — Evidence configuration limits which staff and risk registers appear in each audit.

## Sprinto capabilities used

Zones (add-on) · multiple risk registers · policy registers · vendor registers · RBAC and custom roles · multiple Trust Centers · reports per zone.

## Outcome

One platform instance for the group, with segregated accountability and evidence per entity while retaining shared controls.
