# UC-16 — Custom obligations (Unified Commitments)

## Situation

Obligations arrive as customer contracts, security exhibits, regional regulations, or internal standards that are not in the standard framework catalog.

## Actors

- GRC lead
- Legal / sales (contract owners)
- Control owners

## Trigger

Enterprise contract with security addendum, new regulation (e.g. DORA, NIS 2), internal policy baseline, industry standard.

## Flow

1. **Ingest obligation** — Upload regulation or contract; Sprinto interprets and structures it into machine-readable controls (Unified Commitments add-on) or build a custom framework manually.
2. **Map** — Cross-map to existing CCF/SCF controls; only net-new criteria require work. Bring Your Own Controls (Growth).
3. **Monitor** — Attach automated or workflow checks to new controls; scoring methods for consistent evaluation.
4. **Maintain** — Keep obligations current as rules change; report compliance per commitment.

## Sprinto capabilities used

Unified Commitments · custom frameworks · Bring Your Own Controls · Secure Controls Framework support · programmable monitors · reports.

## Outcome

Contractual and regulatory commitments managed with the same controls, evidence and dashboards as standard frameworks.
