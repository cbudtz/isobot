# UC-14 — AI governance and shadow AI

## Situation

Employees adopt AI tools and the company builds AI features; ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act create governance obligations; customers ask about AI usage.

## Actors

- Security / GRC lead
- Product and data owners of internal AI systems
- Employees using third-party AI tools
- Legal / privacy

## Trigger

Shadow-AI detection, new AI feature, customer or regulator inquiry, framework adoption (ISO 42001).

## Flow

1. **Discover** — Automated and shadow-AI discovery across SSO, browser and vendor signals; user-level visibility.
2. **Inventory** — Register internal and third-party AI systems with custom fields and ordering.
3. **Assess** — Configurable AI risk factors and automated risk scoring; standard assessments.
4. **Diligence** — Request documents and questionnaires; review vendor AI security posture.
5. **Govern** — Findings and remediation tasks; violation detection and investigation; policies mapped to ISO 42001 / NIST AI RMF / EU AI Act.
6. **Report** — AI systems report; trust-center disclosure.

## Sprinto capabilities used

AI Systems module · shadow-AI discovery · AI risk scoring · due diligence workflows · violations monitoring · AI Governance add-on · ISO 42001 framework.

## Outcome

Live AI registry with risk classification and defensible governance evidence as AI regulation matures.
