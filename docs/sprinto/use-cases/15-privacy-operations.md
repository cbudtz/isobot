# UC-15 — Privacy operations (DPIA, data rights)

## Situation

GDPR/UK-GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA and ISO 27701 require privacy impact assessments, handling of data subject requests, and public privacy artifacts.

## Actors

- DPO / privacy lead
- Product owners
- Legal
- Data subjects (external)

## Trigger

New processing activity, high-risk feature, data subject request, privacy framework adoption.

## Flow

1. **DPIA** — Create DPIAs using workflows and forms; approvals; map identified risks to the risk register and controls.
2. **Frameworks** — Enable GDPR / ISO 27701 / CCPA; cross-mapped controls via CCF.
3. **Data rights** — DSR request portal on the Trust Center; handle submissions and track completion.
4. **Public artifacts** — Monitors verify privacy page, ToS and related pages exist on company products.
5. **Vendors & AI** — Privacy considerations flow into vendor and AI system due diligence.

## Sprinto capabilities used

DPIA module · privacy frameworks · Privacy Management add-on · Trust Center DSR portal · product web-property monitors · risk mapping.

## Outcome

Structured privacy evidence integrated with the wider GRC program rather than a separate tool.
