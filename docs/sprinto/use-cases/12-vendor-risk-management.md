# UC-12 — Third-party / vendor risk management (TPRM)

## Situation

Vendors and SaaS tools are adopted continuously; frameworks and customers require inventory, risk tiering, due diligence and ongoing monitoring. Sprinto calls this "Autonomous TPRM".

## Actors

- Procurement / IT
- Security reviewers
- Business owners requesting vendors
- Vendors (via portal)

## Trigger

New vendor request, SSO/shadow-app discovery, periodic reassessment, breach alert, audit requirement.

## Flow

1. **Discover** — Vendor discovery via Google Workspace / SSO, browser, MDM and API; library or CSV import; employee vendor requests via intake.
2. **Inventory** — Vendor profiles with owners, data touched, access level, lifecycle stage; vendor registers per zone.
3. **Assess** — Configurable risk parameters and tiered scoring; AI evaluation of SOC 2 reports and security documents; Risk Pulse signals; map risks.
4. **Diligence** — Request documents and questionnaires; vendor self-service portal; custom onboarding/diligence workflows (Enterprise TPRM).
5. **Monitor** — Breach monitoring, periodic reassessment cadence, continuous posture signals.
6. **Act** — Findings and tasks tracked to verified completion; vendor reports.

## Sprinto capabilities used

Vendors module · vendor discovery integrations · AI vendor document review · vendor portal · Risk Pulse · breach monitoring · vendor registers · Enterprise TPRM add-on.

## Outcome

Live, owned vendor inventory with proportionate review depth; third-party risk evidence always current.
