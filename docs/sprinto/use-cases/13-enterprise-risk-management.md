# UC-13 — Enterprise risk management

## Situation

Leadership, auditors and insurers require a current risk register linked to controls, vendors and assets, with treatment tracking and quantification.

## Actors

- CISO / risk owners
- Risk committee and approvers
- Employees reporting risks (intake)
- Auditors

## Trigger

Annual/periodic risk assessment, new systems or vendors, incidents, failing controls, board reporting.

## Flow

1. **Identify** — Pre-built ISO 27005-aligned risk library with control mapping; risk intake workflow for employee submissions; link assets and vendors.
2. **Score** — Default or custom/weighted scoring methods; customizable scales; inherent and residual risk.
3. **Treat** — Define treatments, map controls, create and track treatment tasks; multi-approval workflows.
4. **Operate** — Multiple registers by unit/function; zones; owner accountability and escalation.
5. **Monitor** — Continuous risk monitoring dashboard recalculates from live signals (checks, vendors, scans); heatmaps with thresholds; trend and historical reporting.
6. **Report** — Risk reports and dashboards for leadership.

## Sprinto capabilities used

Risks module · risk registers · scoring methods · risk intake · Continuous Risk Monitoring · Enterprise Risk Management add-on (aggregation, inheritance, formula builder).

## Outcome

Risk posture that reflects current signals rather than a quarterly workshop; traceability from risk to control to evidence.
